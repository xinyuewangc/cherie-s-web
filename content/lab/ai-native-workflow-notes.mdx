AI-native UX is not a layer of chat on top of a product. It is a change in how the work moves: research becomes searchable, options become explicit, and decisions leave a better trail.

## Loop

1. Capture raw material from product questions, user signals, constraints, and interface evidence.
2. Ask AI to make structure visible: actors, surfaces, states, risks, and missing information.
3. Convert the structure into design moves that can be tested in the interface.
4. Save the reasoning next to the artifact, not in a separate graveyard of meeting notes.

## Design rule

AI should reduce the distance between ambiguity and a useful artifact. It should not replace taste, accountability, or the designer's responsibility to make the product legible.
