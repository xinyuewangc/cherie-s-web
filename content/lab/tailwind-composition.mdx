Tailwind is useful when it becomes a grammar for composition, not a dumping ground for decoration.

## What matters here

The portfolio leans on a few repeated decisions: constrained content width, quiet borders, large vertical spacing, compact controls, and hover states that reveal affordance without shouting.

## Pattern

Use layout utilities for the system. Use tokens for theme. Use component boundaries for meaning.
