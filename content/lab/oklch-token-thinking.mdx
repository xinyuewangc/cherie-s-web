OKLCH is useful because it lets color work feel less accidental. Lightness, chroma, and hue can be adjusted with clearer intent.

## In this portfolio

The palette stays mostly neutral. The accent is soft enough to support the grid, terminal surfaces, hover states, and active navigation without turning the site into a single-hue theme.

## Rule

Use color to show state and rhythm. Let typography and layout carry the argument.
