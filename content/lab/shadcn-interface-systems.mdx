The best component system is not the one with the most variants. It is the one that lets teams express intent with the least confusion.

## Why primitives

Primitives make the boundary between product logic and visual language easier to maintain. A button, command menu, dialog, and card do not need to feel branded by force. They need to feel dependable.

## Portfolio use

For this site, shadcn/ui gives the portfolio a product-tool feeling: command menu, dock navigation, dialog behavior, dark mode, and composable content cards.
